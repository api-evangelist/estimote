Finally, smartphones can be really smart. The little computer in our pockets could connect to a server on the other side of the world, but it didn’t really know much about our surroundings or the context we were in… That reality changed in 2013 when Apple released iOS7 and opened up the Core Location API. This allowed iPhones to detect the presence of low power Bluetooth beacons.
